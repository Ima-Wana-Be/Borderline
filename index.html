Borderline

Four-colour map puzzles with capitals and market towns. Each puzzle has exactly one solution.

Rules. Colour every region 1 to 4 so no two regions sharing a border match. Filled regions are given. ★ Capitals: the four starred regions are all different. ◇ Market towns: the four regions meeting at a diamond are all different. Pick a colour below (or press 1–4, 0 to erase), then tap regions.
